Meal Delivery & Food Service Nutrition Audits

An independent, dietitian-led review of your menus, recipes and processes — giving your business, your funding partners, and your customers confidence in every meal you send out.

Who This Is For

Built for providers feeding vulnerable Australians

If your meals reach NDIS participants, aged care clients, or anyone with swallowing difficulties or complex dietary needs, the margin for error is small — and the compliance expectations from your funding partners are real.

Meal delivery companies

Home-delivered meal services supplying NDIS participants, Support at Home clients, or the broader community.

Aged care & disability providers

Residential, community and in-home food service operators needing independent nutrition sign-off.

Catering & food service operators

Corporate or contract caterers who need to demonstrate nutrition and food safety credibility to win or retain contracts.

Why It Matters

The risk of getting it wrong

Regulatory & compliance risk

Meals need to meet the Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ) Code, and — for texture-modified and thickened-fluid meals — the IDDSI (International Dysphagia Diet Standardisation Initiative) framework used across NDIS and aged care settings. Getting texture levels or allergen labelling wrong isn't just a compliance gap, it's a genuine safety risk for people with swallowing difficulties.

Trust with funding partners & customers

NDIS Support Coordinators, aged care providers and families increasingly ask meal providers to demonstrate independent nutrition oversight before they'll recommend or contract you. A written audit report from an Accredited Practising Dietitian gives you something concrete to show them.

What's Included

A practical, independent review — not a tick-box exercise

Menu & recipe nutritional analysis

Review of nutritional adequacy, portion sizing and menu variety against relevant Australian dietary guidelines.

Texture-modified & dysphagia-safe compliance

Alignment check against the IDDSI framework for texture-modified foods and thickened fluids.

Allergen labelling & food safety documentation

Review of allergen declarations and supporting documentation against the FSANZ Food Standards Code.

NDIS & aged care standards alignment

Assessment against the expectations of the NDIS Practice Standards and Aged Care Quality Standards relevant to food service.

Written audit report

A clear, prioritised report you can act on immediately — and share with funding partners, referrers or your own quality team as evidence of independent review.

The Process

How an audit runs

1. Scoping Call

We discuss your menus, client base and the specific standards you need to meet.

2. Document & Menu Review

Our Dietitian reviews your menus, recipes, allergen documentation and processes.

3. Findings & Report

You receive a written report with clear, prioritised, practical recommendations.

4. Follow-Up Support

We're available to help you action recommendations or re-review after changes.
