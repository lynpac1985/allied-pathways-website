What We Offer

Allied health services built around your goals

Every service is delivered by AHPRA-registered or accredited clinicians experienced in disability and home care support.

Nutrition Support (Dietitian)

Personalised dietary plans for swallowing difficulties, weight management, tube feeding and complex health conditions.

Learn more

Speech Pathology

Support for communication, swallowing (dysphagia), social skills and assistive technology for all ages and abilities.

Learn more

Exercise Physiology

Tailored, evidence-based exercise programs to build strength, mobility and independence at home or in the community.

Learn more

Nursing Services

Clinical nursing support including wound care, medication management, continence care and complex health needs.

Learn more

Occupational Therapy

Functional capacity assessments, home modifications, assistive technology and daily living skills support.

Learn more

Meal Delivery Company Audit

Dietitian-led nutrition audits for meal delivery providers — menu compliance, texture-modified meal safety and NDIS/aged care standards.

Learn more
Funding Made Simple

NDIS, Support at Home, Medicare and more — we work with all of them

Not sure how your funding works, what's included, or how to get started? Beyond NDIS and Support at Home, we also see clients funded through Medicare, private health insurance, DVA, TAC/WorkSafe and private fee-for-service. Our team helps you understand your options and handles the paperwork so you can focus on your goals.
