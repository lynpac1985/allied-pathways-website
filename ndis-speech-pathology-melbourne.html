Who this helps

Communication & swallowing support

Our Speech Pathologists support participants with communication difficulties, social skills development, augmentative and alternative communication (AAC), and dysphagia (swallowing) assessment and management — working closely with families and support teams.

  • Speech, language & communication assessment
  • Augmentative & Alternative Communication (AAC)
  • Dysphagia (swallowing) assessment & management
  • Social communication & group programs
  • Training for families, carers & support workers
Working Together

Why families choose us for dysphagia support

Swallowing difficulties (dysphagia) don't sit neatly under one discipline. Our Speech Pathologist works alongside our Dietitian on texture-modified diets and mealtime safety — so nutrition and swallowing plans are coordinated, not siloed.

Getting Started

What happens after you refer

1. Referral

You, a family member, or a professional submits a referral online or by phone.

2. Welcome Call

Our intake team confirms your funding and availability within 2 business days.

3. Initial Assessment

Irene meets you at home, in the community, or via telehealth to understand your goals.

4. Ongoing Support

Regular sessions, clear reporting, and progress reviewed against your goals.

Funding

How much does an NDIS Speech Pathologist cost?

As at 1 July 2026, the NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits set a maximum price of $193.99 per hour (metro) for Speech Pathology — we charge in line with this published limit. Speech Pathology is most commonly funded under Capacity Building (Improved Daily Living), and we work with self-managed, plan-managed and NDIA-managed participants. Support at Home, Medicare (GP Chronic Condition Management Plans), DVA and private health extras cover may also apply.

Price limits change each financial year — see the current published rates at ndis.gov.au/providers/pricing-arrangements, or read our plain-English funding guide for how each pathway works.
