NDIS Allied Health Across Melbourne's Major Suburbs

Dietetics, Speech Pathology, Exercise Physiology, Nursing and Occupational Therapy — delivered at home, in the community, or via telehealth, across Melbourne's busiest and most populous suburbs.

South-Eastern Suburbs

Glen Waverley, Mount Waverley, Oakleigh, Chadstone, Springvale & Dandenong

Our team is based in Glen Waverley, so this is our home turf, along with neighbouring Mount Waverley and Oakleigh. We also cover the Chadstone activity centre, and the multicultural communities of Springvale and Dandenong, where our Dietitian's low-FODMAP and PEG-feeding expertise and Mandarin-speaking clinicians can help.

Eastern Suburbs

Box Hill, Burwood & Blackburn

Box Hill is a major hub within our east-of-the-city coverage for Exercise Physiology and Nursing support. We also cover Burwood and Blackburn, including clients connected to local aged care facilities, day programs and schools.

Knox & Outer East

Rowville & Ferntree Gully

Rowville, one of Melbourne's larger outer-eastern suburbs, and Ferntree Gully are both part of our home-visit coverage — for Speech Pathology support for younger NDIS participants as well as Nursing visits for older clients.

Western Suburbs

Footscray, Sunshine, Werribee & Point Cook

In Melbourne's fast-growing west, we cover inner-west hubs Footscray and Sunshine, along with the growing communities of Werribee and Point Cook. For clients further out, telehealth keeps Nutrition Support and Speech Pathology accessible without the travel.

Northern Suburbs

Preston, Reservoir, Coburg & Epping

Preston, Reservoir and Coburg are part of our northern coverage, alongside the growing community of Epping. Home visits and telehealth are both available across the north, so distance from our Glen Waverley base doesn't have to mean a longer wait for support.
