## CRITICAL — fix first

### 1. Fabricated testimonials carry real legal risk
The three homepage testimonials ("J. Marsh", "R. Kaur", "T. Nguyen") are AI-drafted, not from real clients. Beyond the Australian Consumer Law concern already flagged earlier in this project, two more specific things surfaced this pass:
- One is attributed to an "NDIS Support Coordinator" — the exact referrer persona the site is trying to win.
- Nursing and Occupational Therapy are AHPRA-regulated; the **Health Practitioner Regulation National Law s133 bans testimonials in advertising regulated health services**. The "R. Kaur" quote ("Mum's care is in good hands") reads close to a clinical testimonial.
- **Fix:** remove the block now. Real testimonials can go back in once collected via the existing `testimonial.html`/QR system — just add guidance there to steer people toward service-experience comments (communication, punctuality) rather than treatment outcomes.

### 2. Live placeholder text on 5 indexable pages
- `blog-post.html` — the site's only article literally reads "[Placeholder article content — replace with your own copy before publishing.]"
- `blog.html` — 14 placeholder markers; a visible dev note about CMS integration
- `team.html` — Occupational Therapist card still `[Practitioner Name]` / `[Placeholder bio]`
- `services.html` — "(Placeholder copy — please confirm scope and wording.)" on the Meal Delivery Audit section
- `contact.html` — "Map placeholder — Glen Waverley, VIC" instead of a real map
- **Fix:** hide/remove unfinished sections rather than publish scaffolding. `curl -s <url> | grep -ic placeholder` should return 0 on every page.

### 3. No canonical host — 4 live copies of the entire site
`http://alliedpathways.com.au`, `https://alliedpathways.com.au`, `http://www.alliedpathways.com.au`, `https://www.alliedpathways.com.au` all return `200` with byte-identical content — no redirect anywhere. On-page `<link rel="canonical">` tags point to `https://www` but nothing enforces it server-side.
- **Fix:** add a `.htaccess` (or SiteGround Site Tools rule) forcing all traffic to `https://www.alliedpathways.com.au`. This is a single fix that also resolves related findings #8 and #13 below — see the bundled fix note at the end.

### 4. No page can win a real discipline search
Checked against 9 real search queries ("NDIS dietitian Melbourne", "NDIS speech pathologist Melbourne", etc.) — **96% of competing results are dedicated single-discipline pages** ("NDIS Dietitian Melbourne", with their own URL). This site has one shared `services.html` with ~90 words per discipline. A prospective client or Support Coordinator searching for a specific service is very unlikely to find this site.
- **Fix (bigger effort, highest strategic value):** build 5 dedicated pages — `ndis-dietitian-melbourne.html`, etc. — each ~600-800 words: who it's for, what a first appointment looks like, the clinician, funding/pricing, FAQ, referral CTA. Keep `services.html` as the hub linking to them.

## HIGH

5. **No `Person` schema for real team members.** Muhe, Irene, Jack, Jeina have names/credentials/photos in the HTML but zero structured data — a real E-E-A-T gap for a health site. (Sample JSON-LD in the schema agent's notes — happy to draft and wire this in.)
6. **6 of 7 blog "Read article" links point to the same wrong article.** Every teaser card on `blog.html` except one links to `blog-post.html`, which is about NDIS plans, not whatever the card promised.
7. **Zero security headers.** No HSTS, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, CSP, or Referrer-Policy — notable for a site handling health-adjacent contact/referral data.
8. **404.html still not wired to the server.** Confirmed again live — SiteGround's generic 404 still serves. (Same `.htaccess` fix as #3.)
9. **Factual error: site says Speech Pathology is AHPRA-regulated.** It isn't — Speech Pathology Australia (CPSP) self-regulates it. The team page gets this right ("Certified Practising Speech Pathologist"); the blog post contradicts it.
10. **Contradictory registration claim.** Homepage stat says "100% NDIS & Support at Home Registered"; the footer badge says "Associated Support at Home Provider" — those aren't the same status. Recommend swapping the stat for something verifiable (e.g. the NDIS Provider Number).
11. **Team credentials aren't verifiable.** First names only, no registration numbers — can't be checked against the AHPRA/Dietitians Australia/SPA/ESSA public registers. Add a surname or reg number per clinician.
12. **Referral form is invisible to anything that doesn't render iframes.** `refer.html`'s only real content is a cross-origin Splose iframe with zero fallback link or native `<form>`. Add a plain-text link to the Splose form URL as a fallback.
13. **`contact.html` labels the physical suite address as "PO Box."** It's not a PO Box — just a labeling bug.
14. **No local landing page despite being Glen Waverley-based.** 80% of local-intent search results are suburb-specific pages; this site only has `service-area.html` listing suburbs as plain text.
15. **Homepage hero image has no loading priority; Google Fonts render-blocks every page.** Add `fetchpriority="high"` to the hero image, consider self-hosting fonts.
16. **Real differentiators are nearly invisible.** PEG feeding, sports dietetics, low-FODMAP/IBS, Mandarin-speaking staff — all buried in one bio sentence each, absent from services.html, schema, and metadata. These are genuine competitive advantages that neither Google nor AI search can currently "see."
17. **Outdated funding terminology.** "Chronic Disease Management (CDM) Plan" was replaced by GP Chronic Condition Management Plans on 1 July 2025; "myplace portal" was replaced by the "my NDIS" app; Home Care Package language should reflect the 1 Nov 2025 Support at Home transition.

## MEDIUM

- Privacy Policy and Feedback & Complaints footer links both go to `contact.html` — no real dedicated pages exist (a Privacy Act / NDIS complaints-handling gap, not just SEO)
- `sitemap.xml` has no `lastmod` dates on any of its 10 URLs
- No outbound citations to primary sources (ndis.gov.au, health.gov.au) anywhere on the funding guide
- Readability runs above the likely audience's comfort level in several places (team.html scores FKGL 16 — aim for ≤9)
- Generic, interchangeable "about us" copy that could describe any competitor
- No case studies or first-hand experience signals anywhere on the site
- Team page photos are 900×900 but displayed much smaller — unnecessary byte weight
- No `width`/`height` attributes on any `<img>` sitewide (CSS `aspect-ratio` mitigates most layout-shift risk already, but it's not full coverage)
- Referral page is thin (110 words) with a 2-business-day SLA vs. competitors advertising 24h/1-day
- `geo` coordinates in schema only carry 4 decimal precision (~11m); tighten once a Google Business Profile pin exists
- IndexNow protocol not implemented (low cost, minor benefit for Bing/Yandex)
- No llms.txt / Markdown delivery (optional — Google Search ignores it, no major AI engine has confirmed using it to select citations)

## LOW / no action needed

- `MedicalBusiness` schema is duplicated verbatim across 10 files rather than templated — a maintenance-drift risk, not an error
- FAQPage schema on the funding guide is fine to leave as-is (Google retired FAQ rich results site-wide in May 2026, so it's informational only — don't expand or remove it)
- Testimonials correctly do **not** carry `Review`/`AggregateRating` schema — leave this alone, adding it would risk a structured-data spam flag under Google's "self-serving reviews" policy
- "Melb" stat abbreviation looks like a placeholder — spell out "Melbourne"
- Team page hero has no CTA button (the persistent header CTA covers this, low urgency)
- Muhe's beach-photo framing is inconsistent with Irene/Jack's studio headshots — a polish item, not a defect
- **Footer trust badges (NDIS/APD/Centre for Care) appeared blank in the visual agent's automated screenshots — I checked this live in a real browser and they render correctly.** It was a `loading="lazy"` timing artifact of the screenshot tool, not a real bug. No action needed.
- Backlink profile is thin, but that's expected and unremarkable for a site live only a few weeks — one legitimate dofollow link from the Centre for Care parent site is confirmed, nothing toxic

## One `.htaccess` fixes three Critical/High items at once

Findings #3 (no canonical host), #8 (404 not wired up), and #7 (no security headers) are all solvable with a single `.htaccess` file deployed via the existing SSH/rsync pipeline:
- `RewriteEngine On` rules forcing everything to `https://www.alliedpathways.com.au`
- `ErrorDocument 404 /404.html`
- `Header set` directives for HSTS, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy

I can write and deploy this whenever you give the go-ahead — it's the single highest-leverage fix available on the technical side.
