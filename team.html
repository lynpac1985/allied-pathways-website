Meet the Team

A small, dedicated team of registered and accredited allied health clinicians who know Melbourne's NDIS and Support at Home community well.

Director, Dietitian & Practice Manager
Accredited Practising Dietitian

Muhe

Experienced in nutrition support for NDIS participants and older Australians, with a special interest in swallowing-safe meal planning, PEG (enteral tube) feeding, sports dietetics for active clients, and low-FODMAP diets for clients with IBS. As Director and Practice Manager, Muhe also leads the practice day to day and is often the first friendly voice you'll speak to — coordinating referrals, scheduling and funding questions for clients and families. Mandarin speaking.

Speech Pathologist
Certified Practising Speech Pathologist

Irene

Passionate about communication access, helping NDIS participants and older Australians be understood and stay connected with their families and communities. Experienced in supporting clients with speech, language and swallowing needs, including AAC (augmentative and alternative communication), and works closely with families and support teams to build practical, everyday communication strategies.

Exercise Physiologist
Accredited Exercise Physiologist (ESSA)

Jack

Focused on building strength, confidence and independence through tailored, achievable exercise programs at home and in the community. Mandarin speaking.

Registered Nurse
Registered Nurse (AHPRA)

Jeina

Registered Nurse with a background in clinical and complex care, dedicated to providing safe, compassionate, person-centred support for NDIS participants and older Australians. Focused on building genuine relationships with clients and their families, and helping people feel confident and supported in their own home.
