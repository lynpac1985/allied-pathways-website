About Allied Pathways

A dedicated allied health team, backed by the experience and governance of the Centre for Care group.

Our Story

Allied health, built around the person

Allied Pathways was established to give NDIS participants and Support at Home clients across Melbourne easier access to high-quality Nutrition Support, Speech Pathology, Exercise Physiology, Nursing and Occupational Therapy — delivered where it suits them, by clinicians who take the time to understand their goals.

We believe good allied health care starts with listening. Every plan we write is shaped around what matters to the person in front of us — their routines, their family, and their own definition of progress.

Our Parent Company

Part of the Centre for Care group

Allied Pathways operates under Centre for Care, an established provider of in-home care and support services. Being part of the Centre for Care group means our clients benefit from strong governance, robust quality and safeguarding systems, and a coordinated approach when someone needs both allied health and broader in-home care support.
