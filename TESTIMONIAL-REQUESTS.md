# Testimonial Request Templates

Ready-to-send templates for collecting real testimonials. Send shortly after a positive interaction — right after a good session, a milestone, or a warm comment someone makes in passing.

Always confirm exactly how they want to be identified before publishing (full name, first name + initial, role only, or anonymous).

---

## 1. Client / Family Member — Email or Text

Use after a good session, a milestone, or when someone says something positive in passing.

> Hi [Name],
>
> It was really great [seeing you today / hearing how things are going with the exercise program / etc.] — thank you for sharing that with us.
>
> We're updating our website and would love to include a short quote from families we work with, if you'd be comfortable. It doesn't need to be long — even a sentence or two about your experience with Allied Pathways would mean a lot.
>
> If you're happy to, just reply with a few words and let me know how you'd like to be credited (e.g. your first name and initial, "Parent of NDIS participant," or anonymous is totally fine too).
>
> No pressure at all if you'd rather not — just thought I'd ask!
>
> Thanks again,
> [Your name], Allied Pathways

**Follow-up once they reply with a quote:**
> Thank you so much — that means a lot to us. Just to confirm, are you happy for us to publish this on our website as: "[however they said they want to be credited]"? Just want to double check before it goes up.

---

## 2. Support Coordinator / Referrer — Email

Easier ask since it's a professional relationship, not a personal endorsement.

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for continuing to trust us with your clients' care — it's genuinely appreciated.
>
> We're refreshing the testimonials on our website and would love to include a short quote from Support Coordinators we work with regularly, about your experience referring to Allied Pathways. A sentence or two on communication, reliability, or outcomes would be perfect.
>
> Happy to credit you by name and title, or just "NDIS Support Coordinator" if you'd prefer to stay anonymous — whatever you're comfortable with.
>
> Would you be up for sending a few lines through?
>
> Thanks again,
> [Your name], Allied Pathways

---

## 3. GP / Care Partner — Email

Underused source — worth trying even if response rate is lower.

> Hi Dr [Name],
>
> Thank you for your referrals to Allied Pathways — we value working with your practice.
>
> We're building out the testimonials section of our website and would welcome a short quote from referring GPs about your experience with our team, if you have a moment. Even a line or two on communication or clinical outcomes would be great.
>
> Happy to credit you and your practice by name, or keep it general (e.g. "Referring GP") — whichever you prefer.
>
> No obligation at all, but would be much appreciated if you're able to.
>
> Kind regards,
> [Your name], Allied Pathways

---

## Also worth doing: ask for a Google review at the same time

If someone agrees to a testimonial, ask if they'd also be willing to post it (or a short version) as a Google review on your Business Profile — one ask, two outcomes:

> If you're also open to it, popping a short version of that onto our Google Business page would help other families find us too — here's the link: [Google review link]. Totally optional, but hugely appreciated if you're able to.
